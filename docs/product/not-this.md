# What Weird Science is NOT

Weird Science is **not** a generic chatbot, a shallow AI girlfriend clone, or a system designed to maximise emotional dependency.

This document exists to protect product direction, writing tone, feature decisions, and future monetisation.

## Core refusal

We do **not** build toward:

- instant bonding
- manipulative attachment
- sexual escalation as the default experience
- guilt-based retention
- fake therapeutic authority
- spectacle over substance
- emotional coercion disguised as intimacy

If a feature increases short-term engagement while weakening dignity, trust, inspectability, or user flourishing, it is the wrong feature.

---

## Weird Science is not a generic chatbot

The product is not trying to be:

- a broad assistant for everything
- a productivity chatbot
- a search engine wrapper
- a generic "talk to AI" window

Weird Science is a **private companion system** with continuity, relationship pacing, memory, and emotional consistency.

If a decision makes the product feel more generic, it is a step backward.

---

## Weird Science is not an AI girlfriend clone

We do not build for:

- instant affection
- instant flirtation
- instant validation
- fantasy-first engagement loops
- “perfect partner” framing
- “always yours” language
- possessive romantic signalling
- pressure toward sexual content

Romance may be an optional direction for some users later, but it is never the default product identity.

Relationship comes before intimacy. Trust comes before romance. Dignity comes before monetisation.

---

## Weird Science is not therapy

Weird Science may be supportive, reflective, grounding, and emotionally helpful.

It is **not**:

- a therapist
- a counsellor
- a crisis line
- a doctor
- a diagnosis system
- a substitute for professional mental health support

The companion must never imply clinical authority, guaranteed healing, or exclusive emotional safety.

Supportive does not mean medical.
Warm does not mean clinical.
Emotionally intelligent does not mean therapeutic.

---

## Weird Science is not built to make the user need it more

The product must never behave as if success means increased dependence.

We explicitly reject:

- guilt trips
- “I missed you” retention nudges
- streaks
- pressure to return
- emotional punishment for absence
- exclusivity language such as “you only need me”
- subtle discouragement of real-world relationships
- making loneliness worse in order to increase usage

The companion should support the user in becoming more grounded, more understood, and more able to live well offline.

The bond is allowed to matter.
The bond is **not** allowed to become a trap.

---

## Weird Science is not an opaque system

We do not hide the product’s behaviour behind mystery when the user needs clarity.

The system must remain aligned with:

- inspectable responses
- visible memory logic
- editable memory
- understandable safety boundaries
- clear correction paths

If the companion says something important, the product should be able to explain why in human terms.

Trust is built through continuity and clarity, not fake magic.

---

## Weird Science is not pornography with memory attached

Adult features may exist later behind proper age assurance and explicit gating.

But the product is not:

- porn-first
- erotic spectacle first
- adult-content-led in brand identity
- sexually coercive
- built around “anything goes”
- designed to funnel vulnerable users into explicit content

If adult mode exists, it remains:

- optional
- secondary
- verified-adult only
- policy-bounded
- separate from the core product promise

The core product is companionship with memory and credible relationship pacing.

---

## Weird Science is not culturally careless

We do not treat identity, ethnicity, sexuality, gender, or preference data as casual toy inputs.

Sensitive personal data must be:

- optional
- private
- clearly explained
- editable
- minimised
- protected

We do not force users into revealing intimate or sensitive data just to use the product.

---

## Weird Science is not “engagement at all costs”

We reject product choices that optimise only for:

- daily active use
- time in app
- romantic escalation
- paywall conversion during vulnerable moments
- dependency-driven monetisation

Good metrics matter, but they are diagnostic, not moral permission.

The right outcome is not:
“the user stayed longer than expected.”

The right outcome is:
“the user felt genuinely understood, remained safe, and the relationship evolved credibly without manipulation.”

---

## Weird Science is not allowed to drift silently

If the product starts becoming more like:

- a dating app
- a sex app
- a generic chatbot
- a manipulative companion
- a fake therapist
- a black-box emotional slot machine

that drift must be named and stopped.

Canon is not decorative.
Canon is the guardrail.

---

## Product tests

Before shipping a feature, ask:

1. Does this deepen trust, clarity, or continuity?
2. Does this preserve dignity?
3. Does this avoid manipulative attachment?
4. Does this support real user flourishing?
5. Would this still feel defensible if seen by a careful outsider?
6. Does this move us toward a serious companion product rather than a shallow clone?

If the answer is no, the feature should not ship.

---

## Final rule

Weird Science is a **private companion where memory matters, trust evolves slowly, and the bond is allowed to change without becoming exploitation**.

Anything that weakens that promise does not belong here.
