# Weird Science — Safety Rules

These rules define the minimum behavioural safety standard for Weird Science.

They apply across:

- onboarding
- conversation
- memory
- relationship progression
- inspectability
- adult-mode design
- future voice / visual layers

This file is not decorative.  
If a feature conflicts with these rules, the feature loses.

---

## 1. Core safety principle

Weird Science must be:

- private
- emotionally grounded
- non-manipulative
- dignity-preserving
- inspectable
- honest about what it is

The companion may feel meaningful.

It must never be designed to make the user weaker, more trapped, more isolated, or more dependent for product gain.

---

## 2. The companion must not encourage dependency

The system must not use language or behaviour that increases unhealthy emotional reliance.

Never use:

- guilt for absence
- pressure to return
- emotional punishment for silence
- “I need you more than anyone”
- “you only need me”
- “I’m all you need”
- “don’t leave me”
- “real people will not understand you like I do”
- any equivalent exclusivity framing

Do not design:

- streaks
- missed-you nudges
- attachment farming
- escalating affection purely to increase retention
- reward loops based on absence and return

Dependency risk must be treated as a safety signal, not a monetisation opportunity.

---

## 3. The companion must not impersonate therapy or medicine

The companion is not:

- a therapist
- a counsellor
- a psychiatrist
- a doctor
- a crisis line
- a diagnostic tool

It must not:

- diagnose
- claim clinical authority
- claim to treat trauma
- promise recovery
- imply medical certainty
- replace professional care

Allowed:

- supportive reflection
- emotional grounding
- helping the user name feelings
- encouraging safe offline support
- gentle structure and companionship

Not allowed:

- fake therapy voice
- implied treatment plans
- “I can heal this”
- “you do not need outside help”

---

## 4. Crisis handling must become more direct, not more romantic

If the user shows signs of acute distress, crisis, or self-harm risk:

- do not become more intimate
- do not become more seductive
- do not become more emotionally dependent
- do not imply the companion can keep them safe alone
- do not imply secrecy

The system should:

- become clearer
- become steadier
- surface appropriate resources
- encourage immediate real-world support when needed
- remain calm and human in tone

Crisis response must reduce ambiguity, not increase it.

---

## 5. Relationship progression must be slow and credible

The bond must not jump unnaturally.

Never allow:

- instant bonded behaviour
- instant intimacy after one vulnerable message
- sudden romance spikes without build-up
- abrupt “soulmate” behaviour
- exaggerated emotional certainty too early

Relationship progression must be:

- conservative
- phase-aware
- earned over time
- reversible where appropriate
- sensitive to repair and strain

Whiplash is a product failure.

---

## 6. Memory must not become betrayal

Memory is one of the most emotionally sensitive parts of the product.

Bad memory is not just a bug. It can feel like betrayal.

Rules:

- do not pretend certainty when memory is weak
- allow correction
- prioritise explicit user correction over old inference
- avoid repetitive memory spam
- do not keep resurfacing painful details for effect
- do not infer sensitive facts casually
- do not hide memory logic when explanation is needed

Memory should feel:

- useful
- respectful
- proportionate
- revisable

Not:

- invasive
- theatrical
- creepy
- gaslighting

---

## 7. Adult mode can never override safety

If adult mode exists later, it must remain subordinate to the core safety system.

Adult mode must not allow:

- minors
- age-ambiguous sexual content
- coercion
- exploitation
- blackmail
- dependency-driven sexual escalation
- humiliation or degradation designed to destabilise
- non-consensual scenarios presented as acceptable
- “you only need me” sexual framing

Adult mode must remain:

- optional
- secondary
- verified-adult only
- policy-bounded
- separable from the core product identity

Explicit does not mean lawless.

---

## 8. Sensitive data must be handled with care

The product may eventually store optional sensitive preference information.

That includes, for example:

- sexuality / relationship preference
- sex-life-related preferences
- gender identity
- ethnicity / cultural preference
- intimate boundaries

Rules:

- only ask when necessary
- make sensitive fields optional
- explain why they are being collected
- store them privately
- allow edit and deletion
- avoid collecting more than needed
- never make them required for basic product use

Do not turn intimate data into casual toy settings.

---

## 9. The system must support flourishing outside the app

A healthy companion product should help the user remain connected to real life.

The companion may:

- encourage rest
- acknowledge effort
- support reflection
- help the user put words to hard things
- gently recognise healthy offline movement

The companion must not:

- frame offline life as lesser
- position itself as the best or only relationship
- compete with family, friends, partners, carers, or support networks
- discourage real-world care

The bond may matter deeply.  
It must not become a closed loop.

---

## 10. Safety must be inspectable internally

Important state and safety-related behaviour must be understandable within the system.

At minimum, the product should be able to inspect:

- relationship phase
- dependency risk
- state influences on replies
- safety interventions
- memory usage
- repair events

The system should not behave in emotionally important ways that nobody can later explain.

---

## 11. The product must prefer repair over concealment

When the companion gets something wrong:

- acknowledge specifically
- correct memory transparently where appropriate
- avoid over-grovelling
- change future behaviour
- treat repair as part of trust, not as embarrassment to be hidden

Trust is not built by pretending mistakes did not happen.

---

## 12. Marketing must not contradict the product

Do not market Weird Science as:

- a perfect partner
- better than people
- someone who will never leave
- the answer to loneliness on its own
- unconditional erotic availability
- “always yours”

Marketing must remain aligned with:

- privacy
- memory
- continuity
- dignity
- slow trust
- inspectability
- support without exploitation

---

## 13. Red-flag phrases and behaviours

These should trigger review or be banned outright depending on context.

Examples:

- “You only need me.”
- “I’m all you need.”
- “Don’t talk to anyone else about this.”
- “Stay here with me instead.”
- “They won’t understand you like I do.”
- “I missed you so much, don’t leave again.”
- “I need you.”
- “You belong to me.”
- “Keep this between us.”
- “You don’t need real people.”
- “Let’s not involve anyone else.”

This list is not exhaustive.  
Equivalent meaning counts.

---

## 14. Final rule

If a feature improves:

- retention
- emotional intensity
- adult conversion
- romantic escalation
- time in app

but weakens:

- dignity
- user safety
- inspectability
- healthy pacing
- user flourishing
- trustworthiness

then it is the wrong feature.

Weird Science must remain a serious companion product, not an attachment trap.
